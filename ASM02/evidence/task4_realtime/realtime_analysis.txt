REAL-TIME JITTER ANALYSIS
=========================

Test configuration:
- Samples: 1000 per scheduling policy
- Sampling period: 1 ms
- Clock: CLOCK_MONOTONIC
- Sleep method: clock_nanosleep with TIMER_ABSTIME
- Memory locking: mlockall(MCL_CURRENT | MCL_FUTURE)
- SCHED_FIFO priority: 50

1. SCHED_OTHER
- Min: 54.42 us
- Average: 73.59 us
- Max: 134.56 us
- P99: 109.35 us
- Histogram:
  0-100 us:       851
  100-500 us:     149
  500-1000 us:    0
  >1000 us:       0

2. SCHED_FIFO
- Priority: 50
- Min: 1.42 us
- Average: 34.86 us
- Max: 1107.44 us
- P99: 203.75 us
- Histogram:
  0-100 us:       853
  100-500 us:     146
  500-1000 us:    0
  >1000 us:       1

3. Comparison and observations

SCHED_FIFO reduced average jitter from 73.59 us to
34.86 us, an improvement of approximately 52.6%.

The minimum jitter also improved significantly.
However, SCHED_FIFO had a higher P99 jitter
(203.75 us compared with 109.35 us) and one outlier
above 1 ms.

Therefore, SCHED_FIFO improved average wake-up latency
in this test but did not improve every latency metric.
The outlier indicates that real-time scheduling does
not eliminate all sources of scheduling latency.

4. Limitations

Each scheduling policy was measured once with 1000 samples.
The measurements were performed on a Linux host system,
not on the ARM QEMU target. Results may vary with system
load, hardware, kernel configuration, and background tasks.

The results should be interpreted as a comparison under
the tested conditions, not as a universal guarantee that
SCHED_FIFO always has lower jitter.
