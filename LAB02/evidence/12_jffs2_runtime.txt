Conclusion:
  JFFS2 image was successfully written to the NAND simulator,
  mounted as JFFS2, verified after unmount/remount, and confirmed
  in the raw NAND dump by the JFFS2 magic bytes 0x85 0x19.

Note:
  Persistence was verified across unmount/remount within the same
  nandsim session. This does not claim persistence across QEMU reboot,
  because nandsim storage is simulated in RAM.
