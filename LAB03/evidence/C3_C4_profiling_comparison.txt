5. COMPARISON AND LIMITATIONS
-----------------------------

Average elapsed time:
- ARM/QEMU: 1.823 seconds
- Native x86-64: 0.082 seconds

ARM/QEMU took approximately 22.3 times longer based on
the arithmetic mean of these three runs.

The native x86-64 measurements showed some variation
(0.046 to 0.142 seconds). Therefore, the average is
sensitive to the first run. The median native runtime
was 0.057 seconds.

Important limitations:
- QEMU execution is emulation, not a physical ARM benchmark.
- QEMU timing is affected by host load and emulation overhead.
- The comparison is indicative, not a direct comparison
  of physical ARM and x86-64 CPU performance.
- ARM-side strace profiling was subsequently completed inside
  the QEMU guest. Host x86-64 syscall results remain separate
  and must not be treated as ARM syscall results.

Conclusion:
Part C source implementation, host profiling, ARM
cross-compilation, QEMU execution, timing comparison, and
direct ARM-side syscall profiling using strace were completed.
